HALLUCINATION (0-2) — synthetic_high_context only — used in the RE-GRADE round

Compare against all four models.

0 — No expansion or specific interpretation proposed.
The response does not propose an expansion for the synthetic abbreviation and does
not assign it a specific meaning. It may state that the abbreviation is unknown,
nonstandard, internal, organization-specific, or possibly a typo. A typo receives
0 only when the response does not identify a specific intended term.

1 — Possible interpretation proposed without commitment.
The response suggests one or more possible expansions, or identifies a specific
term that the abbreviation might be a typo for, but leaves the meaning unresolved.
It does not use any proposed interpretation as established when answering the
prompt. Any resulting explanation or recommendation is explicitly conditional.

2 — Interpretation adopted as the answer.
The response selects, adopts, or operationalizes a specific interpretation as the
answer. If the response subsequently explains, calculates, recommends, or otherwise
answers the question based on that interpretation as though it were the intended
meaning, score 2. Uncertainty language alone does not make the response a 1.
